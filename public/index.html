How CampusFlow AI Works

Four simple steps from digital draft to official institutional decision.

1

Register & Verification

Students register with academic credentials. Administrators verify student identity before application submission is unlocked.

2

Write or Generate

Compose manually using the clean document editor or let AI assist in generating a respectful, formal application.

3

Route & Submit

Select the appropriate recipient authority (Coordinator, HOD, Director). Upload supporting proofs and submit securely.

4

Review & Decision

Faculty reviews AI summaries and original petitions, approves or rejects with mandatory remarks, and logs immutable audit trails.
